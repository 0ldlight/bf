# BinFlow 研发入口 / Development entry

按 [接续研发](docs/P0-HANDOFF.md) 读取当前需求、架构、计划、能力矩阵与研究索引；遵循 [研发规范](docs/ENGINEERING-CONVENTIONS.md)。已确认需求无需重新访谈。
Follow the continuation guide to load the current contracts and follow the engineering conventions. Do not reopen confirmed requirements.

后续研发按 [REA 参与研发](docs/ENGINEERING-CONVENTIONS.md#rea-参与研发--rea-in-development) 主动使用 REA 核实涉及发布制品或原版运行行为的兼容性疑点，并将证据转为行为契约与回归测试。
Use REA proactively under the linked workflow when compatibility questions depend on shipped artifacts or reference runtime behavior; turn the evidence into behavioral contracts and regression tests.

任务状态、审批与交付沿用现有接续流程；研究原始资料保持私有，公开内容只保存可公开的摘要、结论与限制。
Keep the existing task, approval and delivery workflow. Preserve raw research privately and publish only releasable summaries, findings and limitations.
